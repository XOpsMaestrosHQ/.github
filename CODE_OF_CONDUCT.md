# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review carefully, and keep this
organization's public-facing profile and branding accurate and representative of the team.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work.
- Treat branding, copy, and public-facing content with care - this repo is the first thing many
  people see about XOps Maestros.
- Ask for clarification when a change or decision is unclear.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Adding secrets, credentials, or internal-only details without approval.
- Dismissing accuracy, accessibility, or brand-consistency concerns without discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, reject, or request changes to contributions that violate this standard.
Security-sensitive concerns should be escalated through the approved internal team channel.
